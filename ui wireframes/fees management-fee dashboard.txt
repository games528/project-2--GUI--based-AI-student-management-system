THIS IS THE MAIN SCREEN FOR TRACKING STUDENT FEES, PAYMENTS, PENDING AMOUNTS, AND PAYMENT HISTORY.

# PURPOSE

Admin can:

1. View total fees
2. Track paid/pending fees
3. Search students
4. Filter fee records
5. Record payments
6. View payment history
7. Generate receipts
8. See overdue fees
9. Export fee reports
